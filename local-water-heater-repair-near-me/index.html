What happens during service and what to ask

Start with the basics: explain whether you have a tankless or conventional heater and what has changed. The service professional can assess the unit and discuss repair or replacement options based on what they find. If replacement is recommended, ask what the proposed work includes. If a repair is suggested, ask which issue it addresses and whether additional work might be needed.

Use these points to compare providers:
- Check that the provider lists service for your type of heater.
- Ask for a plain explanation of the recommended work and estimate.
- Confirm the available appointment window, including whether same-day service is possible.
- Compare repair and replacement options based on the unit’s condition, not just the initial symptom.

You may hear a few common terms. A conventional water heater stores heated water in a tank. A tankless water heater heats water as it is called for. Installation is setting up a unit, while maintenance refers to service that helps keep it operating properly.
